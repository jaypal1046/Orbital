Privacy Policy for Orbital

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Orbital ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. Orbital is architected as a local-first application designed to operate without central servers or tracking infrastructure.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our own servers.

  • API Keys: Your LLM API keys (e.g., OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Together, Fireworks, Gemini) are stored locally on your device using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and are only sent directly to the respective API provider you configure.
  • Conversations & Voice: Conversation history and voice audio are processed locally or sent directly to your chosen AI provider endpoint. No chat logs are sent to Orbital developers.
  • Device Permissions: Permissions such as Microphone (audio input), Overlay (companion floating window), and Usage Stats (safety monitoring) are utilized strictly on-device to enable the respective core features.

3. Third-Party Services

When you interact with AI features, queries are transmitted directly to the third-party AI provider you choose in Settings. Each provider governs data handling according to their own privacy policies.

4. Security

All sensitive configuration data is encrypted at rest on your device using hardware-backed keystores where available.

5. Open Source & Transparency

Orbital is open source. You can inspect the complete source code and verify our privacy and security claims directly on GitHub.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via GitHub Issues.
