Privacy Policy for Orbital

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

Orbital ("we", "our", or "the app") is a local-first AI companion and device automation system for Android. Orbital operates without central tracking servers or developer-hosted databases. User-requested AI features and the optional paired Laptop Bridge can transmit data off the device as described below.

2. Categories of Information Processed

  • API Credentials & Keys: Your third-party AI provider API keys (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, Together AI, Fireworks, Google Gemini) are stored locally in your device's hardware-backed private storage using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences. Keys are never transmitted to Orbital developers.
  • Conversations & Chat Sessions: Chat messages, generated responses, and prompt history are persisted exclusively in a local on-device SQLite/Room database. No chat logs are collected or backed up to external servers.
  • Voice & Audio Input: Audio recording via the microphone occurs strictly on-demand when you interact with the voice input button. Orbital does not perform passive background audio recording or continuous acoustic surveillance. Audio data is streamed directly to your configured speech-to-text or AI provider endpoint.
  • Media & Documents: Images, PDFs, and files attached to chat queries are processed in memory and sent directly over TLS to your selected AI provider for multi-modal inference.

3. Zero Accessibility Service Architecture (Banking & UPI Safe)

Orbital does not register, request, or run any Android Accessibility Service. This guarantees that banking apps, Google Pay, PhonePe, and payment systems operate without interruption or suspicious activity warnings. Device actions are performed dynamically via standard Android Intents and rootless ADB Wireless (Shizuku) shell scripts pursuant strictly to user commands.

4. Remote Laptop AI Bridge & Cryptographic Security

Orbital features an optional wireless Laptop AI Bridge allowing verified desktop development environments (e.g. Antigravity IDE, Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT MCP) to dispatch remote automation commands to your device over local Wi-Fi, USB reverse forwarding, or encrypted WebSockets:

  • Bitcoin-Grade Cryptographic Authentication: Every bridge connection requires explicit user pairing authorization and utilizes 256-bit HMAC-SHA256 session signatures.
  • Tamper Proof: Any unauthenticated or unsigned remote command is dropped immediately with a security alert.

5. Third-Party AI Service Providers

When you send queries, requests are routed directly from your device to the third-party AI provider API you have configured in Settings. These communications are encrypted in transit via standard TLS 1.3. Each third-party provider processes data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/privacy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
  • Groq: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
  • Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

6. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Required for voice input when the microphone button is pressed.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Required for rendering the optional floating mascot overlay.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required for automated status alerts and scheduled briefings (Android 13+).
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & INTERNET: Required for connecting directly to your chosen AI model endpoints and local bridge.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Used strictly on-device to pause assistant overlays during payment or banking app usage.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Because Orbital stores all data locally on your device, you have complete sovereignty over your data:

  • Instant Data Purge: You can wipe all chat history, cached models, and stored API keys at any time via App Settings → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Export & Transparency: You can view, export, and copy all your conversation logs directly from the in-app chat interface.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Orbital is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

9. Open Source Transparency

Orbital is open-source software released under the MIT License. The complete source code is publicly auditable on GitHub to verify all privacy, security, and data handling claims.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in updated application releases and documented in our public repository.

11. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please submit an issue on our official GitHub repository: https://github.com/jaypal1046/Orbital.
