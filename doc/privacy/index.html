1. Zero-Telemetry Client-Side Architecture

Orbital is architected from the ground up to respect complete user data sovereignty. Unlike cloud-centralized assistants, Orbital executes locally on your Android device without telemetry servers, advertising monitors, or central profile databases.

  • Local SQLite Database: Conversations, logs, and mascots reside exclusively in local encrypted sandboxes on your phone.
  • Hardware Keystore Storage: Custom API credentials (e.g. Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenAI) are secured via androidx.security.crypto.EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by Android Keystore hardware keys.
  • No Middleman Proxy: Requests dispatch directly from your client device to your selected AI provider via secure TLS 1.3 HTTPS.

2. Android System Permissions & Purpose

  • Accessibility Service: Optional screen automation reads visible text and controls to carry out your requests. AI screen analysis may send screen information to your selected AI provider. Optional Laptop Bridge inspection sends a snapshot to your paired laptop, where AI tools may process it. You can disable screen access in Android Settings.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only during active voice chat. Audio is transcribed in real-time RAM and is never recorded to disk or sold.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables the interactive floating companion mascot and overlay HUD upon user request.
  • Foreground Service: Keeps the floating mascot active and performs user-scheduled background alert notifications via WorkManager.
  • Internet Access: Connects directly to configured AI provider endpoints.

3. Third-Party Provider Direct Disclosures

Your requests route directly to the model endpoints you enable. Please review their respective privacy notices:

  • Google AI Studio / Gemini
  • Groq Cloud
  • OpenAI
  • Cerebras Systems

4. Data Erasure & Statutory Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, you have unconditional rights to access, port, or erase your data. Because all storage is local, clearing application storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all databases and keys instantly.
