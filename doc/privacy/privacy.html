Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Orbital: Autonomous AI Mascot & Companion mobile software application ("Application", "Service", "Orbital", "we", "us", or "our"), authored and maintained by Jayprakash Pal.

Executive Privacy Summary: Orbital is engineered as an offline-first, client-side executive AI companion. We do not operate user telemetry servers, we do not log or commercialize your prompt histories, and your private third-party API credentials never touch any middleman server.

1. Overview & Scope

This policy details the operational privacy standards governing Orbital in compliance with global privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement.

2. Zero-Telemetry & Client-Side Architecture

Unlike conventional cloud-centric AI wrappers, Orbital enforces a strict zero-telemetry, on-device data sovereignty model:

  • No Central Database: Orbital does not maintain remote user databases or identity tracking servers.
  • Direct Endpoint Transmission: Prompts, context histories, and assistant configurations communicate directly between your device and the AI model endpoint you configure (e.g. Google Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama).
  • No Advertising SDKs: The Application contains zero commercial tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, or third-party advertising monitors.

4. Keystore, Cryptographic Auth & Bridge Security

When you provide custom API credentials (such as Google AI Studio, Groq Cloud, or OpenAI API keys), the keys are saved using Android's official androidx.security.crypto.EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by the hardware-level Android Keystore System. Keys are decrypted exclusively in memory at the exact moment of HTTP dispatch and are never logged or exported.

Remote Laptop AI Bridge: Remote automation commands received from desktop IDEs (Antigravity, Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT MCP) require explicit local Wi-Fi / USB device authorization and 256-bit HMAC-SHA256 cryptographic session signatures. Any unsigned or unauthorized remote command is dropped immediately.

5. Android System Permissions Justification & Accessibility Disclosure

Orbital requests minimal system permissions strictly tied to user-initiated functionality:

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): Optional, user-enabled service (OrbitalAccessibilityService) used strictly to inspect on-screen UI node hierarchies (view IDs, text labels, bounds) and simulate user-directed taps/gestures for device automation and remote AI testing. In compliance with Google Play User Data policies:
    • Accessibility snapshots can contain visible text and controls from other apps. Orbital processes them on-device for automation. If you request AI screen analysis, screen information may be sent to your selected AI provider. Optional Laptop Bridge inspection sends the snapshot to your paired laptop, where AI tools may process it. Avoid sensitive screens during automation.
    • The service is disabled by default and can be revoked at any time via Android Settings → Accessibility.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required strictly when the user activates the microphone for real-time speech transcription. Audio streams are ephemeral and never stored on disk or shared with external analytics.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Enables the optional floating interactive mascot companion and floating HUD chat bubble to stay visible on screen upon user request.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Ensures the interactive companion overlay remains responsive during active use and facilitates user-scheduled background monitoring tasks.
  • Alarm & Wake Lock (SET_ALARM / WAKE_LOCK): Allows the execution of autonomous background cron monitoring and reminder notifications scheduled by the user via Android's WorkManager.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Enables direct HTTPS communication with configured AI provider endpoints and local bridge connections.

7. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights

Because all personal data resides entirely on your client device, you possess total statutory control:

  • Right to Access & Portability: All conversation data and session transcripts can be reviewed directly in the Application.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Deleting a conversation in the app or tapping "Clear Data" in Android System Settings permanently purges all local databases and cryptographic keys.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or commercialize any personal data under CCPA/CPRA regulations.

8. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure

All data persists on-device solely for as long as you maintain the application on your hardware. Uninstalling Orbital or clearing application storage through Android Settings causes an immediate and irrecoverable deletion of all SQLite databases, encrypted preferences, and cache files.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Orbital is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.
